Eyedle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Eyedle (“the app”) is designed to be completely private. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. Eyedle does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and your break history (used only to show your streak and statistics) are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use “Reset statistics” in Settings.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps — to show the eye-break reminder over whatever you are using.
  • Notifications — to show an ongoing status while protection is active.
  • Run at startup / foreground service — to reliably time your breaks.
  • Vibrate — optional haptic cue at break time.

None of these permissions are used to collect or share data.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
